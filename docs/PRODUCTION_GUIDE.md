# Production guide

## 1. Lock the narration first

The narration is the master clock. Generate the final voice at the requested speed before editing visuals. Do not stretch the completed mix later; regenerate word timestamps if the voice changes.

## 2. Separate visuals into three classes

1. **Real UI:** landing pages, Studio, results, onboarding.
2. **Editorial AI visuals:** abstract systems, monetization structures, premium conceptual B-roll.
3. **Evidence:** authorized revenue screenshots and testimonials.

Use real UI for factual product claims, AI visuals for abstract explanations, and evidence immediately after revenue or success claims.

## 3. Opening retention

Avoid a static image in the first 10 seconds. Start with a fast real-page scroll or an actual UI action. The page should move on frame one while the narration delivers the hook.

## 4. Dynamic CTA recipe

Use a 5–7 second action sequence:

1. scroll toward the CTA;
2. decelerate near the target;
3. fade and pop the button once;
4. move the cursor to the button;
5. show one click ripple;
6. reveal the exact CTA copy;
7. transition before the motion becomes repetitive.

Avoid constant blinking, rapid neon flashes, and repeated bouncing.

## 5. AI image workflow

Generate only the premium background and objects with GPT Image. Do not ask the image model to render final Korean copy. Add Korean typography afterward with the same font used by the video.

Suggested visual language: black, midnight navy, restrained violet, champagne gold, glass panels, realistic depth, no logos, no watermarks.

## 6. Proof workflow

- Use only authorized screenshots.
- Match approximate revenue categories when exact amounts are not required.
- Blur personal information.
- Keep the original screenshot visible inside a frame.
- Add a short category label and disclaimer outside the screenshot.
- Never place proof randomly over unrelated feature narration.

## 7. Captions

- Reserve the bottom 110 px exclusively for captions.
- Show one line at a time.
- Target 18–22 Korean characters and 2.0–2.7 seconds per cue.
- Derive cue boundaries from word timestamps, not estimated sentence length.
- Keep all other text and UI above the safe area.

## 8. Avatar

Generate one full-length lip-synced presenter video from the final narration. Mask it to a true circle, then add it in the last composition pass. Keep the face inside the circle and avoid covering proof amounts or CTA buttons.

## 9. QA

Extract frames from at least:

- 3 seconds: opening motion;
- first proof;
- CTA click moment;
- first Studio scene;
- every proof category;
- final resource CTA.

Also listen at scene boundaries and compare caption onset to narration onset.
